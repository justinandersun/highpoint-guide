Arkansas's mountains are among the oldest in North America, and its main ranges run east-west rather than north-south. Mount Magazine sits between the two highland regions, the Ozarks to the north and the Ouachitas to the south, which helps explain its mesa-like appearance. Mount Magazine is a flat-topped plateau with a sandstone cap rimmed by steep rock cliffs, featuring two peaks: Signal Hill and Mossback Ridge. Signal Hill is the highest peak in Arkansas. Geologically, Mount Magazine belongs to the Frontal Ouachita Mountains and rises above the Arkansas River Valley, about 120 miles northwest of Little Rock.

Situated in Mount Magazine State Park above the Arkansas River Valley (and surrounded by the Ozark National Forest), Mount Magazine-Signal Hill is approximately 17 miles south of Paris on Scenic Highway 309 (Mount Magazine Scenic Byway).

## Recommended Route
We recommend the <a target="_blank" href="https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/arkansas/signal-hill-trail">Signal Hill Trail</a>.

The Signal Hill Trail is a well-groomed, gentle forest walk to the 2,753-foot summit, where you'll find a 400-square-foot stone map of Arkansas. The trail connects Cameron Bluff Campground with the Mossback Ridge Trail and the Lodge at Mount Magazine. The park lists the trail at 1.5 miles; the loop from the High Point Trailhead shown above is about 1.8 miles round trip with roughly 260 feet of gain.

If you have extra time, the nearby Benefield Loop is a pleasant side trip. It does not reach the summit, but it passes the site of Benjamin Benefield's 19th-century homestead, where WPA/CCC crews later built picnic facilities, and loops through mixed pine and hardwood forest to a wildlife pond.

## Regulations
No permits are required. There are fees for the campground if you are staying overnight. Rangers encourage climbers at Mount Magazine to register at the visitor's center for free.

## Lodging & Camping
Mount Magazine State Park offers 18 campsites. Campers must register at the Visitor Center before occupying a site. For more information on camping accessibility, visit the park website or call the park headquarters at 479-963-8502. The [Cove Lake Recreation Area](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/233834) is a great alternative if the Mount Magazine campground is busy.

## FAQs

### What is the best time of year to hike Mount Magazine?
The best time to hike Mount Magazine is in the spring and fall when temperatures are mild, and the foliage is most beautiful. Summer can be hot, and winter may bring snow and ice, making the trails more challenging.

### How do I get to the trailhead for the Signal Hill Trail?
From Fort Smith, take I-40 east to exit 35, then follow Route 23 south to Paris. From Paris, Mount Magazine is 17 miles south on Route 309. From Little Rock, take I-40 west to Russellville, then Route 7 south to Route 27 south, Route 10 west, and finally Route 309 north. The High Point Trailhead for the Signal Hill Trail is well-marked off Route 309 near the Lodge and Cameron Bluff Campground.

### What amenities are available at Mount Magazine State Park?
Mount Magazine State Park offers a variety of amenities, including 18 campsites, hiking trails, a pavilion and picnic area with restrooms, scenic overlooks, a visitor's center with an exhibit gallery and gift shop, and various recreational activities like rock climbing, horseback riding, biking, ATV trail riding, and hang gliding.

### Are pets allowed on the trails?
Yes, pets are allowed on the trails at Mount Magazine State Park, but they must remain leashed. Make sure to clean up after your pets to keep the park clean and enjoyable for everyone.

### Can I camp anywhere in Mount Magazine State Park?
Camping is only allowed in designated campsites. Campers must register at the Visitor Center before occupying a site. For more information on campsite availability, it's best to contact the park headquarters.

### Are there any special events at Mount Magazine State Park?
Mount Magazine State Park hosts various events throughout the year, including the annual "Mount Magazine International Butterfly Festival." The park staff also presents regular interpretive programs. Check the park's event calendar for more information.
