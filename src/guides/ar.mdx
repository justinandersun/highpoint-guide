Arkansas's mountains are some of the oldest in North America, and unlike most U.S. ranges, they run east to west. Mount Magazine stands between the state's two highland regions, the Ozarks to the north and the Ouachitas to the south, and it looks more like a mesa than a peak: a broad, flat-topped plateau capped with sandstone and ringed by steep cliffs. Two summits rise from the plateau, Mossback Ridge and Signal Hill, and Signal Hill is the highest point in Arkansas. Geologists group Mount Magazine with the Frontal Ouachita Mountains, towering over the Arkansas River Valley about 120 miles northwest of Little Rock.

Signal Hill is inside Mount Magazine State Park, which is surrounded by the Ozark National Forest. From the town of Paris, drive about 17 miles south on Scenic Highway 309, the Mount Magazine Scenic Byway.

## Recommended Route

The Signal Hill Trail is a well-groomed, gentle forest walk to the 2,753-foot summit, where you'll find a 400-square-foot stone map of Arkansas. The trail connects Cameron Bluff Campground with the Mossback Ridge Trail and the Lodge at Mount Magazine. The park lists the trail at 1.5 miles; the loop from the High Point Trailhead shown above is about 1.8 miles round trip with roughly 260 feet of gain.

If you have extra time, the nearby Benefield Loop is a pleasant side trip. It does not reach the summit, but it passes the site of Benjamin Benefield's 19th-century homestead, where WPA/CCC crews later built picnic facilities, and loops through mixed pine and hardwood forest to a wildlife pond.

## Regulations
No permits are required. There are fees for the campground if you are staying overnight. Rangers encourage climbers at Mount Magazine to register at the visitor's center for free.

## Lodging & Camping
The state park has 18 campsites; check in at the Visitor Center before you set up. For campsite accessibility details, see the park's website or call park headquarters at 479-963-8502. If the park campground is full, the [Cove Lake Recreation Area](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/233834) nearby makes a good backup.

## FAQs

### What is the best time of year to hike Mount Magazine?
The best time to hike Mount Magazine is in the spring and fall when temperatures are mild, and the foliage is most beautiful. Summer can be hot, and winter may bring snow and ice, making the trails more challenging.

### How do I get to the trailhead for the Signal Hill Trail?
Coming from Fort Smith, follow I-40 east to exit 35 and take Route 23 south to Paris, then Route 309 south for 17 miles. From Little Rock, drive I-40 west to Russellville, then take Route 7 south, Route 27 south, Route 10 west, and Route 309 north. The High Point Trailhead for the Signal Hill Trail is well-marked off Route 309 near the Lodge and Cameron Bluff Campground.

### What amenities are available at Mount Magazine State Park?
Besides its 18 campsites and hiking trails, the park has scenic overlooks, a pavilion and picnic area with restrooms, and a visitor center with exhibits and a gift shop. It's also a hub for rock climbing, horseback riding, biking, ATV riding, and even hang gliding.

### Are pets allowed on the trails?
Yes, pets are allowed on the trails at Mount Magazine State Park, but they must remain leashed. Make sure to clean up after your pets to keep the park clean and enjoyable for everyone.

### Can I camp anywhere in Mount Magazine State Park?
No. You can only camp in the park's designated sites, and you'll need to check in at the Visitor Center first. Call park headquarters for current availability.

### Are there any special events at Mount Magazine State Park?
Yes. The park holds events throughout the year, including its annual Mount Magazine International Butterfly Festival, and park staff lead regular interpretive programs. See the park's event calendar for dates.
